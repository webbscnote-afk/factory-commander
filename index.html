Day One: HVAC Company

You don't manage people. You manage signals. Capture them, score them, route them to the right Rig, and grow the company.

How to Play

  1. Capture — signals land in your inbox. Tap one. Red border = emergency.
  2. Score — set Urgency, Value, Risk. Close to the real values = bonus.
  3. Route — send it to the right Rig: Sales, Service, Marketing, Review, Automation.
  4. Build — the Rig creates a response.
  5. Deploy — send it into the world. Service jobs use Capacity for a while.
  6. Measure — see the money, reputation and capacity result.
  7. Feedback — learn why it worked or failed.
  8. Improve — spend revenue on upgrades (🛠 button anytime).

Waiting signals decay and cost Reputation. Reputation 0 = company fails. Balance Revenue, Reputation and Capacity.
